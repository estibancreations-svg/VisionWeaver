# VisionWeaver founding pitch and design baseline
Recorded 2026-10-02 America/Chicago. Status: PITCH APPROVED FOR PRESERVATION; architecture remains a proposal pending the Architect's build approval.

## Original pitch — preserved verbatim
> VisionWeaver is not an image or video generator. It is a continuity-aware production operating system that compiles creative intent, canonical identities, world state, performance direction and technical constraints into reproducible media.

The generated picture or clip is only the output. The real product—the “horcrux”—is the **Canon State Engine** underneath it.

## Original design scope retained
This is a structured preservation of the preceding design, not a verbatim chat transcript. All twenty design areas remain in the revised proposal:
1. A scene is a living state: entities, knowledge, emotion, intent, physical possibility, visibility and permitted change.
2. Avatar performance identity: identity core, physical model, appearance, expressions, voice, behavior, knowledge, emotion, relationships, rights and history.
3. Persistent world: geography, historical date/local time, weather, terrain, architecture, traffic, water, wind, sound, signage, ecology and off-camera space.
4. Scene graph: character/prop/camera/world relationships and their consequences.
5. Shot Contract: source versions, purpose, start/end states, blocking, camera, performance, sound, duration, permitted/forbidden changes, references and acceptance.
6. Continuity Capsule: first/last frame, motion tail, pose, gaze, hand occupancy, props, wetness, world, light, audio and narrative intent.
7. Causality: cause → physical consequence → perception → emotion → performance → camera → audience meaning.
8. Camera as an entity: position, height, rotation, distance, lens, movement, focus, exposure, screen direction and purpose.
9. Voice continuity: permanent identity, delivery, environmental acoustics and final mix as distinct layers.
10. Prop identity and lifecycle: stable IDs, ownership, material, scale, condition, motion and narrative importance.
11. Sound continuity: rain, wind, footsteps, clothing, traffic, dialogue, ambience, music, silence and audio tails.
12. Change control: locked, persistent, scene-local, dynamic, optional and forbidden attributes.
13. Unified UI: Project → Canon → Scene Builder → Performance → Storyboard → Generate → Continuity Review → Assembly → Delivery.
14. Provider translation: compile compatible inputs, record model/settings/task, evaluate, and retry or route based on capability.
15. QC: identity, continuity, technical quality and narrative meaning.
16. Branching: alternatives share parent state; only the approved take advances canon; rollback and comparison.
17. Persistence: durable records/media, visible save/sync state, retries, checksums, recovery and audit.
18. Provenance: delivered film → assembly → shots → tasks → contracts → capsules → asset versions → sources/rights.
19. Economics: forecast cost and continuity risk; preview, approval, final rendering and mastering.
20. Operating system: creative memory, casting, virtual stage, continuity supervision, directing, routing, QC, post-production, provenance and publishing.

## Original proof
Define an avatar once; place it in a persistent world; direct a performance; generate and preserve its ending; continue while changing only intended facts; reload without loss; trace all decisions and deliverables.
The Boy and the Red Balloon is the first proof: one avatar, one principal prop, one world, twelve provisional connected shots, environmental interaction, camera behavior, emotional causality, durable continuity and disclosed AI creation.

## Clarifications controlling the revised proposal
A prompt lock cannot force a probabilistic provider to comply.32 generated views are identity references, not a calibrated3D reconstruction. A frame cannot reveal exact hidden geometry, mass or camera parameters. Seed/task logging aids traceability but does not guarantee rerender reproducibility across provider versions.
The twelve10-second segments are a test scaffold, not an editing rule. “150x” and the later “30” are ambition and revision depth, not measured performance improvements.
See VISIONWEAVER-PRODUCTION-DESIGN-v2.md for30 concrete improvements, evidence, limits and approval gates.
