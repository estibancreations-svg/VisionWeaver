# VisionWeaver Standard: the 360 View Storyboard

**Set by Sire on 2026-09-26. Applies to every new character board and object (prop) board from this date forward.**

## The name

Character boards and object boards are now called **360 View Storyboards**. Title every new one: `360 VIEW STORYBOARD: <NAME>`.

## The one big rule: head to toe, 8 sides

Every 360 View Storyboard shows the **whole subject, head to toe** (for an object: the whole object, top to bottom), from **8 sides**, like a camera walking all the way around it:

| # | View | Camera angle (orbit, seen from above) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Front | 0° |
| 2 | Front-right angle | 45° |
| 3 | Right side | 90° |
| 4 | Back-right angle | 135° |
| 5 | Back | 180° |
| 6 | Back-left angle | 225° |
| 7 | Left side | 270° |
| 8 | Front-left angle | 315° |

"Right" and "left" mean the subject's own right and left.

**Why (Sire):** with all 8 sides, head to toe, the light and the details are tracked better from shot to shot. It works like a turntable photo shoot: once you've seen every side under the same light, nothing about the subject is a guess.

## Also on every board

- **Same light on all 8 views** (one soft key light from the same direction, same backdrop, same exposure), so light can be matched in scenes.
- **Same scale** on all 8 views, with a height marker (for example, `6'2" (188 cm)`).
- **Close-up detail panel:** face (or the object's key surface), hands, and any signature items (jewelry, tattoos, glasses, labels-free markings).
- **Specs panel:** hair, skin, build, wardrobe or materials, in plain words.
- **No real brands, logos or copied designs.** Everything is our own.

## Existing boards

**Keep them as they are.** Sire, 2026-09-26: *"Do not recreate anyone's boards right now. We will use what we have."* The new standard applies to new boards only, unless Sire asks for a rebuild.
