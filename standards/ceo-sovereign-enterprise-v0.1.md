# VisionWeaver Part 3 — CEO Sovereign Enterprise v0.1

**Status:** implementation contract
**Date:** 2026-10-06
**Purpose:** Enterprise command layer for company-wide commercial truth, digital twins, brand governance, global campaign control, approvals, compliance, security, performance intelligence, and executive decisions.

## What Part 3 is
Part 3 is not a larger template library. It is the executive control system above VisionWeaver production.

It governs:
- enterprise truth graph
- product/property/service digital twins
- machine-enforceable brand constitution
- global campaign graph
- regulatory and claims intelligence
- talent/rights/contract intelligence
- enterprise security and access
- executive multi-agent review
- CMGIO performance and investment intelligence
- boardroom-level status and decisions

## Primary users
CEO, CMO, Chief Brand Officer, Chief Growth Officer, Legal/Compliance, Business Unit Presidents, Enterprise Creative Director, Regional Marketing Leads, authorized agencies/vendors.

## Core operating principle
The CEO surface shows decisions, risk, business impact, approval state and portfolio health. It does not expose production detail unless the executive drills down.

## Executive command chain
Enterprise Objective
→ Enterprise Truth Resolution
→ Market / Audience Strategy
→ Campaign Graph
→ Creative / Production Execution in VisionWeaver
→ Multi-layer QC and legal review
→ Executive approval gates
→ Distribution through CMGIO
→ Performance / cost / learning
→ Boardroom reporting

## Enterprise truth graph
Canonical records must be queryable and versioned:
- brands and sub-brands
- products, SKUs, models, floorplans, packages and options
- services and service levels
- properties, buildings, units, rooms and amenities
- markets, regions, dealers and operating locations
- official prices/offers where authorized
- verified claims and supporting evidence
- contracts, licenses, talent usage and expiration
- regulatory requirements
- brand rules
- source provenance and authority

No generated creative may override locked truth.

## Digital twin layer
Enterprise assets may represent:
- products and packaging
- yachts, RVs, vehicles and equipment
- residential/commercial real estate
- retirement/senior living campuses and units
- retail/hospitality environments
- offices, campuses and factories

Digital twins can ingest approved CAD/BIM/floorplans/engineering models/scans/photography/specifications. Creative outputs inherit exact approved twin versions.

## Brand Constitution
Per brand:
- identity system
- approved product presentation
- copy/tone rules
- camera/lens/movement language
- lighting/material rules
- audio/voice rules
- prohibited treatments
- required disclaimers
- approved claims
- localization rules

Compliance is machine-checkable. 'Close enough' is not approval.

## Campaign graph
One master campaign branches by:
- market
- audience
- language
- product/property variant
- channel
- duration/aspect ratio
- dealer/property/local unit
- casting variant

Every derivative retains lineage to the master campaign and locked enterprise truth.

## Enterprise boardroom surface
The default CEO board must answer:
1. What is launching?
2. What is blocked?
3. Why is it blocked?
4. What needs executive approval?
5. What is performing?
6. What is costing money?
7. Which campaigns should scale, pause or revise?
8. What risk exists in legal, brand, rights, data or product truth?
9. What changed since the last executive review?
10. What decision is recommended next?

## Executive status cards
Required top-level metrics:
- Active campaigns
- Launches awaiting approval
- Blocked work
- Rights/claims exceptions
- Digital twins in approved state
- Markets live
- Spend / committed production cost
- Revenue / qualified lead / conversion metric where connected
- Creative fatigue / declining performance alerts
- SLA / system health

## Decision queue
Each item must show:
- decision requested
- business unit
- campaign / product
- reason
- evidence summary
- risk level
- recommendation
- cost / performance effect where known
- approvers
- deadline
- audit trail

No agent may self-approve its own work.

## Security

- SSO/SAML
- SCIM
- MFA
- RBAC + attribute-based access
- project/business-unit compartments
- zero-trust/JIT access
- encryption
- immutable audit history
- legal holds / retention policies
- DLP controls
- customer-managed keys where available
- regional data controls
- agency/vendor scoped workspaces

## CMGIO integration
CMGIO receives only approved campaign packages and returns:
- publishing state
- spend
- engagement
- qualified leads
- conversions
- CPA/CPL/CAC where configured
- revenue/ROAS where available
- comments/messages/trend signals
- scale/pause/retarget recommendations
- creative fatigue alerts

Performance can change rankings and recommendations. It cannot rewrite enterprise truth, brand law, rights or legal constraints.
