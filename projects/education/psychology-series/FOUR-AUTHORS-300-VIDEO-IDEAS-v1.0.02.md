## How to use this catalog

Every entry supplies an original title, explanation angle, concrete animation direction, related concept and a broad source anchor. These are original author-inspired educational ideas, not 75 quotations from each author or an abridgment of their books. The related-concept labels are editorial connections; some are communication or teaching concepts rather than formal psychological constructs.

Use a 45–75 second explainer: situation → pattern → explanation → specific visual change → context → reflection. Use original Avatars with the established VisionWeaver reference and continuity workflow. Source-credit the author inspiration separately from technical psychology evidence. Exact definitions, study claims and quotations need episode-level checking during scripting.

The source anchors below were researched through author-owned sites, primary research, university reporting and official institutes/training organizations. Carnegie’s current training guidance is distinct from his original prose. Several Carnegie pages returned 403 on direct opening, so their anchors rely on indexed excerpts or listings; the full chapter was not obtained. The Frankl Center page contains several authors and should not be treated as a list of Frankl quotations.

Production state: idea and direction drafts only; no provider renders or publication. This catalog supplements the existing series; it does not replace the 152-topic catalog.
