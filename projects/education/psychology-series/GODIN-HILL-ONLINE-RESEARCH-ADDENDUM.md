## Napoleon Hill: additional sayings and ideas

The Foundation attributes this saying to Hill: “Whatever the mind can conceive and believe, it can achieve.” Its [Hill’s Secret article](https://www.naphill.org/focus-instructors/hills-secret/) explicitly rejects the interpretation that merely thinking long enough makes an outcome appear. It discusses taking steps and applying his principles. This is a Foundation instructor’s commentary and attribution, not an independently checked original audio recording.

| Theme | Located source and what it supports | Proposed psychology connection | Animation direction |

| Belief followed by action | [Hill’s Secret](https://www.naphill.org/focus-instructors/hills-secret/) connects the famous saying to steps toward a plan. | Self-efficacy; goal pursuit | A thought bubble becomes a checklist, then one performed action. |

## How these ideas fit the Psychology series

### Original episode hook: Waiting to be chosen

“You keep refreshing the inbox, hoping someone will finally say you are ready. Seth Godin calls for choosing yourself. That does not mean you control who hires you or who likes your work. It means separating their decision from a step you can take. Make one sample. Practice one skill. Ask one specific question. Then use the response to decide what comes next. What could you begin while the outcome is still uncertain?”

Animate an original avatar in a waiting room, then at a small workbench. Keep the employer’s decision visibly separate from the avatar’s action. This is original adaptation, not Godin’s quoted speech. No video render is claimed.
