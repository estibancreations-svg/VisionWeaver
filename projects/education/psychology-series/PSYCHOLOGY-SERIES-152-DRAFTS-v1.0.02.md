## Pilot: From helplessness to hope
A broken elevator is an analogy, not a scientific experiment. The button repeatedly fails, the circuit is repaired, and the character hesitates because of earlier experience. One supported action demonstrates a possible new influence; nothing promises that every life barrier is now controllable.
