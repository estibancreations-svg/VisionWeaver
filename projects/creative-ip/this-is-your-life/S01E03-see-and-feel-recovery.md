**Status:** creator-confirmed story; exact original source/title/subject name not yet recovered.

## Creator-confirmed canon
A serial killer murdered women over a period of years. The squad tracking him is eventually revealed as angels. The television confronts him with his crimes, but he repeatedly tries to watch them again. The Fates issue the judgment: **“See and Feel.”** He is made to become each victim approximately five minutes before he murdered her, sees his former self arrive and kill the body he now occupies, and experiences the victim’s terror, pain and death amplified. The experience repeats victim by victim.
