# Episode 1: Elijah Memorial Options (v1, DRAFT, waiting on Sire's picks)

**Date:** 2026-09-26 · **Status:** ideas only. Nothing here is canon until Sire says yes.

## The idea (Sire's words, summed up)

While the radio plays at the end of PART 2, the people who knew Elijah look over pictures of him and the things he did. It works as our own memorial to him, and it builds the story. The moments in that house can also be a "beacon," meaning things other people could start, too.

**Think of it like a family photo album that gets passed around at a wake.** Each page is one thing Elijah built, and each person holding it has their own memory of that page.

## Why it fits what we already wrote

- **Victor** is the house historian, so he's the natural one to hold the pictures.
- **Amara** scanned Elijah's photographs in 2020, so the pictures already exist in our story.

## New moments we could invent (canon only if Sire says yes)

- **The Porch Light Book:** a guest book for every kid who stayed. One page reads "Marcus, Oct 2006."
- **The Bulb Jar:** burned-out porch bulbs saved in a jar, one for each year the light stayed on.
- **The carvings:** a small carved figure for every kid who left for a new life.
- **The Pay-It-Forward wall:** notes from people who went on to start something of their own. This is the "beacon" idea.

## Safety rules for the pictures

- Every picture is original and clearly made up: no real photos, real people, real banners or real quilt panels.
- The AIDS years are shown with dignity.
- Victor's and Jordan's face boards must be approved before they appear on camera.
