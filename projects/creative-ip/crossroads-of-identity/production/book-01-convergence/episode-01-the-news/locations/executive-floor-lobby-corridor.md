# Location — Executive Floor: Elevator Lobby and Corridor (Atlanta Head Office)

Same building and firm as the glass conference room, Marcus's office and the executive bathroom. Used in Episode 1, PART 2, shots S10–S14: the senior partner sees the clients out, the elevator doors close, and the partner walks Marcus back up the corridor to his own corner office.

**Status:** Built 2026-09-25 for the locked PARTS 1–2. **Pending Sire's visual check** (this sandbox can't open the finished image).

## Design
It was built with the approved conference room plate `40faad4e…` as the reference image, so the materials match exactly: dark walnut paneling, honed white marble, brushed black metal, polished light-grey concrete floor, frosted glass with a geometric privacy pattern, and brass accents. The design draws on the same research as `conference-room-glass-tower.md` and `executive-bathroom.md` (top-tier Atlanta law firm interiors and Class-A towers).

**No real firm name, building, or specific interior is depicted or copied. This is original art. The reception wall is deliberately blank: the firm stays unnamed.**

## Layout (Map 8)
- **Elevator lobby** at the south end. **Two brushed-bronze elevators** on its west wall. A **blank white marble reception wall** on its east side.
- **Corridor** running north. **Glass-front attorney offices** on the east side, whose east windows take the morning light, which glows through their glass fronts. **Walnut paneling with one abstract painting** on the west side.
- **The senior partner's corner office** at the north end, behind a walnut door.
- **Marcus's office and executive bathroom** sit on the west side of the floor (off-frame; PART 3).
- **Camera pins H1–H4:** see `../part-01-02/camera-maps.svg` and `../part-01-02/lock-sheet.md`. H1 is the plate's own camera position.
