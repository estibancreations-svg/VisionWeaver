## Locks
- **Orientation:** the porch runs along the house's east front, with the door centered and the steps directly in front of the door at the end of the walkway from the wrought-iron gate. The porch wraps around to the south side.
- **Elijah's chair:** a well-worn wooden rocking chair just north of the door, **always turned to face the street**, with a small side table and a coffee mug.
- **Porch light (LK-2022-AM):** in late March the sun sits east-southeast. At 9:30 AM it's about 27° high and at 10:00 AM about 33°, low enough to reach under the porch roof to a seated person's face. By 11:00 AM (about 46°) the roof shades the chair. That's why the chapter says "Mid-morning" (changed from "Late morning" in chapter v3).
- **The four creaks:** creak 1 = the bottom step and creak 2 = the top step (Episode 1 PART 3), creak 3 = the board in front of the door (PART 5), creak 4 = unrevealed, saved for a later episode. Reuse the same creak recordings across the series.
