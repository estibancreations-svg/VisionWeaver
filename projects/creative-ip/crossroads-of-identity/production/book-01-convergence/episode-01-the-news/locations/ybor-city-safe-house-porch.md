# Location — Ybor City Safe House: Front Porch and Front Yard

The east-facing front porch and front yard of Elijah Johnson's safe house. The porch is the setting of Episode 1 PART 5 (Marcus's last visit, late March 2022), and the front walk is part of PART 3 (the 2006 arrival). This is a companion to [`ybor-city-safe-house.md`](ybor-city-safe-house.md) (the full house record) and to the Location Bible v4 in Drive (`1vmjZIxORlYy9ay05jmVZHuIRXRFbgt7E`).

## The set house (master reference)
Sire's set house image is the master exterior. Sire holds the file; the production sandbox couldn't upload it to Runway, so each plate uses the canonical exterior `2aa055ef…` (the same design) as its reference image, plus an exact written description of the set house. What must match:
- **Porch:** it runs along the east front and swells into a **rounded bay under the turret**. It has smooth round cream **Tuscan columns**: single ones on brown plinths around the bay, and paired ones on **red-brick piers with grey stone caps** at the entry and corners. The railing is thick cream **turned vase balusters** between chocolate-brown rails, with dark lattice between brick piers under the deck. The ceiling is chocolate brown, and the porch roof is brown shingle following the curve.
- **Entry:** it's **north of the turret, not centered**, under a small **pedimented gable with a carved cream scroll ornament**. Grey concrete steps run between **low red-brick cheek walls with stone caps and white stone urns** holding boxwood. The front door is dark wood with a glass panel and lace curtain, narrow sidelights, and a black wall lantern, with a small square window just past it.
- **Yard:** **no fence and no gate.** Open lawn runs to the sidewalk, with two small pygmy date palms in front of the bay, live oaks with Spanish moss, sabal palms, and a pale blue-grey neighboring house to the north.
- **Elijah's chair:** a weathered brown **wicker** rocking chair at the **north end of the porch** (past the entry), **always facing the street**, with a small side table and a coffee mug.

## Locks
- **Porch light (LK-2022-AM):** in late March the sun sits east-southeast. At 9:30 AM it's about 27° high and at 10:00 AM about 33°, low enough to reach under the porch roof to a seated person's face. By 11:00 AM (about 46°) the roof shades the chair.
- **Late-afternoon look (LK-2006-PM):** the sun is behind the house in the west. The sky glows gold behind the roofline, the east front sits in soft shade, and the spire tip catches the gold. That's exactly what the set image shows.
- **The four creaks:** creak 1 = the bottom step and creak 2 = the top step (PART 3), creak 3 = the board in front of the door (PART 5), creak 4 = unrevealed, saved for a later episode. Reuse the same creak recordings across the series.
- **Camera pins:** P1–P5 (PART 5) and X1–X3 (PART 3). See `../part-05/camera-maps.svg` and `../part-03/camera-maps.svg`.
