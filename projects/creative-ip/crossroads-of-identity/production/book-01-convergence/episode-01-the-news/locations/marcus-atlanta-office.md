# Location — Marcus's Private Office (Atlanta Law Firm)

Same building and firm as the glass conference room and executive bathroom. Appears in Episode 1, PART 3, shots S01–S02: Marcus closes his office door, "managed three steps toward his desk before the pressure in his chest became unbearable," then goes into his private executive bathroom.

**Status:** Built 2026-09-24 at Sire's approval. **Pending Sire's visual check** (this sandbox can't open the finished image).

## Research process
This plate reuses the research already documented for the same firm's executive bathroom (`executive-bathroom.md`). It draws on the design language of top-tier Atlanta law offices and Class-A towers: soft neutral high-contrast palettes, large-scale wood millwork, hospitality-grade lighting, natural stone, and floor-to-ceiling glass with skyline views. The confidential-law-firm design pattern of private support rooms placed off the attorney's office supports giving Marcus a private bathroom door inside his office.

**Story choice:** the office is deliberately impersonal, with no personal photos or keepsakes. That mirrors the chapter's condo detail ("the lack of personal touches was deliberate, another form of compartmentalization").

**No real firm name, building, or specific interior is depicted or copied — this is original art inspired by the documented design language above.**
