# Episode 1 · Narrator Reading Script (v1)

**For:** Sire, recording the Narrator and the Radio Host. **Date:** 2026-09-26

## How to record (5 quick tips)

1. **Find a soft, quiet room.** A closet full of clothes works great: the clothes soak up echo like a sponge soaks up water.
2. **Keep the phone about 6–8 inches from your mouth**, a little off to the side so your "p" sounds don't pop.
3. **Read slowly and warmly**, like telling a story to someone you care about. A "…" means pause for one breath.
4. **One file per cue.** Name them `N01`, `N02` … `N24`, and `RADIO`. If you stumble, just start that cue over. It's fine to keep only the best take.
5. **Keep the files on your phone for CapCut**, and upload them to Runway like you did the pictures, so I can log them and time the subtitles.

**Say it like this:** Ybor = *EE-bor* · Seminole = *SEM-ih-nole* · Esquire = *ESS-kwire* · Montgomery = *mont-GUM-ree*

### N02  (PART 2)

> Marcus needed to respond, to be present, to do his job. The job that had become his armor, his identity, his escape.

### N03  (PART 2)

> Elijah was gone. The man who had opened his door to a terrified fifteen-year-old boy with nowhere else to go. The man who had shown Marcus what it meant to live with integrity when the world denied your right to exist. The man who knew Marcus—the real Marcus—better than anyone in this gleaming tower of glass and ambition.

### N04  (PART 2)

> This was what Elijah had called his "compartmentalization superpower"—the ability to seal off parts of himself so completely that they might as well exist in parallel universes. Right now, Attorney Reynolds was handling a career-making contract negotiation while somewhere in another universe, a former scared kid named Marcus was crumbling under the weight of grief.

### N08  (PART 3)

> Marcus had stood frozen in the doorway, his backpack clutched to his chest, stuffed with a hastily packed collection of belongings—some clothes, his school books, the journal his grandmother had given him that his father hadn't found and burned along with the texts.

> Carlos Vega, the architectural engineer he'd been seeing for the past three months. Casual, discreet, compartmentalized. … *(breath)* … Carlos did not know about the safe house in Ybor City. He did not know about the deacon father and the Christian school teacher mother who had given their son an ultimatum when he was fifteen. He did not know about Elijah Johnson.

### N10  (PART 3)

> He had three more meetings today, a brief to review, and a dinner with potential clients. The will reading wasn't until Friday. He had three days to prepare himself, to reinforce the walls between his worlds, to figure out how to say goodbye to Elijah without letting the carefully constructed life he'd built come crashing down around him.

### N11  (PART 3)

> Marcus straightened his tie, unlocked the bathroom door, and stepped back into the life he had chosen—the life that existed as far as possible from a Victorian safe house in Ybor City and the scared boy who had once found refuge there.

### N12  (PART 4)

> He'd made it through the day. The meetings, the brief, the client dinner—all handled with the precision and focus that had become his trademark. No one had noticed anything amiss, not even his assistant who prided herself on reading his moods.

### N13  (PART 4)

> The condo had been featured in Citygrain last year—"Rising Legal Star's Urban Oasis." The interviewer had praised its "clean lines and uncluttered aesthetic," never guessing that the lack of personal touches was deliberate, another form of compartmentalization.

### N14  (PART 4)

> He hadn't opened it in years, had almost convinced himself he kept it out of simple sentimentality rather than as a lifeline to a self he rarely acknowledged.

### N15  (PART 4)

> Marcus ran his finger over the smooth wood, worn from years of handling during his college and law school days. He'd kept it on his desk then, a small act of courage in otherwise careful years. When had he decided it needed to be hidden away? When had he become so afraid of questions, of connections between his worlds?

### N23  (PART 5)
