## What this is

The scenes show what Marcus **does**. The chapter also tells what he **thinks** and **remembers**, and a camera can't film that. Think of it like a stage play where a storyteller steps out between scenes and says what's going on inside the hero's head. These cues are that storyteller.

**One change to flag:** the script had 4 voice-over lines in Marcus's voice (2 in PART 2, 2 at the end of PART 5). They're written about Marcus in the third person ("Marcus Reynolds, Esquire…"), so they're really narration. This plan moves them to the Narrator (cues N03, N04, N23, N24). ## Subtitles (so viewers can read along)

- **Narrator subtitles look different from dialogue captions.** Narrator: *italic*, warm cream color, soft shadow. Dialogue: plain white. Viewers can tell at a glance who's "talking."
- **Size for vertical video:** up to 2 lines per card and about 32 letters per line. Each card stays up while its words are spoken.
- **Easiest way in CapCut:** run **Auto captions** on your narration track, apply the narrator style, then check the words against the cards below. The cards are already split for you; " / " marks the line break.
- Turn subtitles on for both the short clips and the full YouTube episode. Most people scroll with the sound off.
