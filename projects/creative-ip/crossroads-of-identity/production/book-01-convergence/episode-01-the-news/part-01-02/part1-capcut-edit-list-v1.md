## The three texts (type these exactly, one per bubble)

Use a clean, plain font in dark grey, sized to fit inside each blank bubble on the screen. Pop them in one at a time, about 2.5 seconds apart.

1. `Marcus, it's Victor Mendoza. Elijah Johnson passed last night. Peacefully, in his sleep. Thought you should know.`
2. `Will reading scheduled for Friday at Brennan & Associates. Elijah left specific instructions for you to attend.`
3. `I'm sorry to deliver this news. He spoke of you often.`
