## 0. What PARTS 1 and 2 are

**PART 1 "The Text":** a big-deal meeting in a glass tower. Marcus is in full control, until three buzzes under the table bring the news that Elijah is dead. He can't react. **The short-form clip ends on the water in his glass, trembling as he sets it down.** It's the only crack anyone could see, and nobody does.

**PART 2 "The Performance":** he finishes the deal anyway. The contract is signed, and he gives the senior partner waiting outside the glass the nod: *yes, they took it.* The partner takes over and sees the clients out, while Alvarez and Jayden's manager start talking terms to lock in the rest of the contract. Then Marcus hears he's up for partner. **It ends the moment his boss's door closes, when the smile finally drops.** The click of that door carries straight into PART 3's "the click is too loud."

**The idea behind the shots:** Marcus is always framed against the **bright, gleaming skyline** (his world, the life he built). The guests are framed against the **frosted glass wall** (things you can't see through). The camera stays steady and polished, just like him. The only unsteady things are a phone under a table and the surface of a glass of water.

**How it connects:**
- **Into PART 1:** this is the cold open of the episode.
- **PART 1 → PART 2:** in the full episode, the trembling water cuts straight to Jayden pointing at the contract. The room sound stays thin across the cut.
- **Out of PART 2:** the partner's door click leads into Marcus's own door click in PART 3 S01.

## PART 1 — "The Text"

**INT. GLASS CONFERENCE ROOM — MARCUS'S LAW FIRM, ATLANTA HEAD OFFICE — DAY**

`[S01]` Midtown Atlanta gleams in the January sun, all glass and steel against a cloudless blue sky. Inside the glass room: MARCUS REYNOLDS (32), immaculate, straightens his burgundy tie (his power tie) and leans forward across the polished walnut table. MR. ALVAREZ sits beside him. Across the table: JAYDEN KING and his MANAGER.

`[S02]` Marcus gestures to Alvarez.

MARCUS
What Mr. Alvarez is offering is not just distribution, but a comprehensive marketing strategy tailored specifically to emerging artists from underrepresented backgrounds.

`[S03]` INSERT: his hand slides the bound contract across the walnut toward Jayden.

`[S04]` Jayden, unmoved. His skeptical expression hasn't shifted in an hour.

MARCUS (O.S.)
This clause here guarantees you retain creative control over your content while accessing resources that would typically require years to develop independently.

`[S05]` Jayden and his Manager exchange a look. Jayden reaches for the contract.

`[S06]` INSERT, under the table: Marcus's phone **buzzes** against his thigh.

`[S07]` Marcus doesn't flinch. In negotiations, attention is currency. A second **buzz**, more insistent. A third. Three in a row: his emergency signal with Carlos. Something flickers behind his eyes and is gone.

`[S08]`

MARCUS
*(smooth)*
Take your time reviewing Section 5. It outlines the royalty structure we discussed.

He slides his chair back, slightly.

`[S09]` Low, under the table edge: his left hand rises to the water glass above, while his right hand slips the phone out below.

`[S10]` INSERT, THE SCREEN: a number he doesn't have saved, from the Tampa area code. 10:52 AM.
*Marcus, it's Victor Mendoza. Elijah Johnson passed last night. Peacefully, in his sleep. Thought you should know.*
A second bubble lands.
*Will reading scheduled for Friday at Brennan & Associates. Elijah left specific instructions for you to attend.*
A third.
*I'm sorry to deliver this news. He spoke of you often.*

`[S11]` CLOSE on Marcus. His face does not move. The room's sound thins to the air system and his own breath.

`[S12]` INSERT: he turns the phone face-down on his thigh. Above the table, a careful sip of water. As the glass comes back down to the walnut, **the water's surface trembles.**

**[CUT POINT — short-form clip ends on the trembling water]**

## PART 2 — "The Performance"

**INT. GLASS CONFERENCE ROOM — CONTINUOUS**

`[S01]` Jayden points to something in the contract. His Manager leans in, brows furrowed. Their voices are slightly muffled, far away.

`[S02]`

MARCUS
*(his voice coming from somewhere distant)*
That clause allows for renegotiation after the first million streams. It's unusually artist-friendly for a first contract.

`[S03]` The camera eases in on Marcus's eyes. *(Decision 1)*

MARCUS (V.O.)
Elijah was gone. The man who had opened his door to a terrified fifteen-year-old boy with nowhere else to go.

`[S04]` The room snaps back to full sound.

JAYDEN'S MANAGER
Mr. Reynolds? We asked about the promotional timeline.

`[S05]`

MARCUS
*(sliding seamlessly back)*
Of course. Mr. Alvarez has committed to a three-phase rollout beginning in March.

`[S06]` From the head of the table: he outlines it with practiced precision. Not a tremor, not a crack. *(Decision 1)*

MARCUS (V.O.)
This was what Elijah had called his "compartmentalization superpower."

`[S07]` INSERT: signatures, then handshakes across the walnut.

`[S08]` Marcus's eyes go to the frosted glass wall. Beyond it, in the corridor, a broad silhouette waits: his SENIOR PARTNER, waiting for the word. *(Decision 4)*

`[S09]` Marcus gives him the smallest nod. *Yes. They took it.* The door opens and the Senior Partner comes in, all warmth, to see the clients out himself.

**INT. EXECUTIVE FLOOR — ELEVATOR LOBBY — CONTINUOUS**

`[S10]` The Senior Partner leads the group to the elevators. Alvarez and Jayden's Manager are already talking terms, their heads close together, locking in the rest of the contract. A step behind them, Marcus walks with Jayden and makes the exact right amount of small talk: professional but personable. An elevator arrives.

`[S11]` The bronze doors slide shut on the clients. Marcus's smile holds, perfectly, in the closing gap.

**INT. EXECUTIVE FLOOR — CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS**

`[S12]` Walking north up the corridor, the Senior Partner claps Marcus on the shoulder.

SENIOR PARTNER
Nicely done, Reynolds. Alvarez has been trying to sign King for months. This puts us in a strong position with his whole label.

MARCUS
Thank you, sir. I think it's a solid partnership for both parties.

`[S13]` At the walnut door of his corner office, the Senior Partner pauses.

SENIOR PARTNER
We're discussing partnership track at the executive committee meeting next month. Keep this up, and your name will be on the table.

MARCUS
*(practiced smile)*
I appreciate that, sir.

`[S14]` The partner's door **clicks shut.** Marcus's smile holds for one beat. Then it's gone. He turns toward his own office. *(Decision 3)*

**[CUT POINT — the door click carries into PART 3 S01: "The click is too loud."]**
