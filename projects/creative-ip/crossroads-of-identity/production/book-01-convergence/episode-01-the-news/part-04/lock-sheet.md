## 0. What PART 4 is

The quiet middle of the night. Marcus is home alone, twenty-three floors up, and nobody noticed anything all day. He opens the shoebox he's kept hidden for years, holds the carved figure Elijah gave him, ignores his mother's call, and burns the eggs. There's almost no dialogue. **The objects do the talking.**

**The idea behind the shots:** everything in the condo is cool, clean and impersonal. The only warm light in PART 4 comes from **inside the closet**, where the box is kept, like the past is the one warm thing he owns. When the box goes back on the shelf and the suits slide back over it, the warmth goes with it.

**How it connects:**
- **Into PART 4:** PART 3 ends in the empty daytime bathroom. PART 4 opens on the night skyline, a hard time-jump from day to night.
- **Out of PART 4:** it ends on the exact frame PART 5 uses in the kitchen (Marcus leaning on the counter in the smoke, pin K1). The full episode dissolves from here straight into the porch memory.

**INT. MARCUS'S CONDO — LIVING ROOM — ATLANTA — NIGHT**

`[S01]` The Atlanta skyline at night, twenty-three floors up, filling a wall of glass. A silhouette stands at the window: MARCUS (32), bourbon in hand. His tie and jacket are thrown over the back of the sofa behind him. The room is clean, spare and expensive, and there's nothing personal in it anywhere.

`[S02]` CLOSE on the glass: Marcus's face is a faint reflection floating over the city lights. He made it through the day. No one noticed a thing. Now there's nothing left to hold it back. It isn't sobbing this time, just a hollow ache.

`[S03]` INSERT: on the console table, a glossy magazine. **CITYGRAIN**, our own invented title, with his own living room on the cover under the headline *"Rising Legal Star's Urban Oasis."* His hand turns it face down.

**INT. MARCUS'S CONDO — BEDROOM — CONTINUOUS**

`[S04]` The dark bedroom. The closet door stands open, glowing warm from inside, the only warm light in the condo. Marcus crosses to it.

`[S05]` He sets the bourbon down on the dresser. Then he pushes aside the precise rows of suits, one hand, then both, and reaches up to the highest shelf in the back corner.

`[S06]` INSERT: a plain shoebox comes down into his hands. It's dusty along the lid edge and hasn't been opened in years. He sets it on the bed and lifts the lid.

`[S07]` INSERT: his grandmother's Bible. The cover opens to a handwritten inscription in faded ink: *Marcus Reynolds · Born January 9, 1991*.

`[S08]` INSERT: a photograph. **Marcus at ten** and his father, both grinning, in matching church-retreat T-shirts, his father's arm around him. Marcus's thumb rests at the edge of the photo, but it doesn't touch his father's face.

`[S09]` INSERT: a ninth-grade debate medal on a faded ribbon.

`[S10]` INSERT: a small carved wooden figure of a man reading a book. Marcus lifts it out. His thumb runs over the wood, worn smooth from years of handling.

> ELIJAH (V.O.)
> To remind you that knowledge is power. But wisdom is knowing how to use it.
>
> *(Voice-over only: locked by Sire.)*

`[S11]` CLOSE on Marcus, in the warm closet light, the figure in his hands. He used to keep it on his desk. When had he decided it needed to be hidden away?

`[S12]` His phone RINGS on the bed beside the box. INSERT, THE SCREEN: **Mother**.

`[S13]` Marcus stares at it. Lit from below by the screen, he doesn't move. It rings out and goes to voicemail. The silence afterward is louder than the ringing.

`[S14]` INSERT, THE SCREEN: a text. *Just checking in. Your father and I are praying for you.*

`[S15]` He sets the phone face-down without answering. The figure goes back in the box, and the lid goes on. The box goes back up on the high shelf. His hand pulls the suits back across it, and the warm closet light is hidden behind a wall of tailored wool.

**INT. MARCUS'S CONDO — KITCHEN — CONTINUOUS**

`[S16]` The dark kitchen. He picks the bourbon back up on the way and sets it on the island. He pulls a rarely-used pan from a cabinet and cracks eggs into a bowl. He isn't hungry. He just needs to do something with his hands.

`[S17]` CLOSE on Marcus at the stove, staring at nothing. The eggs hiss. His mind is somewhere else: why would Elijah leave specific instructions for him to be at the reading?

`[S18]` INSERT: the eggs blackening at the edges, a curl of smoke rising into the pendant light.

`[S19]` He snaps back and **curses**, yanking the pan off the heat. He scrapes the blackened mess into the trash. The smoke hangs in the air.

`[S20]` WIDE: he leans back against the counter, exhausted, in the haze under the pendant light. *(This is the same frame as PART 5 S12.)*

**[CUT POINT]** *(In the full episode, this dissolves straight into the PART 5 porch.)*

## 9. Decisions (ALL APPROVED by Sire, 2026-09-25)

1. **Elijah's line:** voice-over only over the carved figure, **or** add a single flashback shot of Elijah pressing the carving into Marcus's hand the day he left for college. That shot would need two new age boards (Marcus at 18 and Elijah at 64). **Voice-over only.**
2. **The magazine beat (S03):** show *Atlanta Modern Living* on the console with his own condo on the cover, and have him turn it face down. It turns the chapter's line about the "deliberately impersonal" condo into a picture. **Approved, with our own invented title: *CITYGRAIN*.**
3. **The Bible inscription:** the chapter says his birth date is written inside. To be 32 in January 2023 and born in 1991 (per Character Histories), his birthday has to fall in **January 1991, on or before the day the story takes place**. **Locked: born Wednesday, January 9, 1991.** The present-day story is set on **Tuesday, January 17, 2023**, and the will reading is **Friday, January 20, 2023**. His mother, who calls three times a year ("Christmas, his birthday, and Mother's Day"), called for his birthday only eight days before. That makes tonight's call even more unusual.
4. **His mother's contact label:** **Locked: "Mother."**
5. **Night relights of the living room and bedroom: approved and built.**
6. **Props: approved and built** (the photograph, the carved figure, and the *CITYGRAIN* cover).
7. **Canon correction (Sire, 2026-09-25):** the Character Histories doc said Marcus reached the safe house in the "summer of 2006." **Locked to fall 2006** to match the approved light (the low fall/winter sun through the south stained-glass window). The Histories doc is updated to v2.
