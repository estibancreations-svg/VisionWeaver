## 0. What PART 5 is

PART 5 is the episode's closing button, and it has two halves:

1. **The porch memory (spring 2022).** Marcus visits Elijah after the Florida bill passes. Elijah challenges him: courtrooms aren't enough.
2. **The present (January 2023, night).** Marcus stands alone in his Atlanta kitchen after burning the eggs. Two voice-over lines close the episode.

**How it rhymes with PART 3:** PART 3 joined the two timelines with a hand match (Elijah's hand on young Marcus's shoulder became Marcus's hands on the marble). PART 5 closes the same way: Marcus's hand on the porch rail becomes his hand on the kitchen counter. The whole episode is framed by hands holding onto something so he doesn't fall.

## 3. Face locks

**The rule is the same as PART 3:** the board is the master. The words describe it and never override it.

### FL-EJ77: Elijah Johnson, 76–77 (porch, March 2022)
- **Board:** `02ba912e-2a28-4cd1-b470-f91213b44b61` (locked).
- **Identity anchors:** broad round face, deep-set close-set eyes, wide flat nose, **mole on his LEFT cheek**, slight gap in his front teeth, square black wire-rim glasses, thin leather bracelet on his right wrist (the same continuity prop as 2006).
- **Age state:** full salt-and-pepper afro (not receding), thick bushy white brows, full close-cropped white beard. The chapter says he looks "older than Marcus had ever seen him," so play him a little more tired than the board: heavier eyelids and a slower hand on the paper. **Don't add any new features.**
- **Wardrobe:** his cream cable-knit cardigan (worn open) over a light blue collared shirt. It's the same "uniform" he wore in 2006.
- **Props:** a newspaper gone "grey and soft in the humidity" in his lap (**only the headline is ever in frame, never a masthead**, so no real paper is named), a coffee mug on the small side table, and a weathered brown **wicker** rocking chair at the north end of the porch, **facing the street** (east), as in the set image.
- **Expressions:** weary at first, then the "long look": steady, not angry, disappointed love. His last line lands quietly, not as a lecture.

### FL-MR31: Marcus Reynolds, 31 (porch, March 2022)
- **Board and anchors:** the same as FL-MR32. Hair lock: short dense coils on top, close-cropped fade on the sides.
- **Wardrobe (LOCKED):** navy suit, white shirt open at the collar, **no tie**, sunglasses folded in one hand. He's dressed as Attorney Reynolds even on a porch; the chapter says he speaks "as Attorney Reynolds rather than as the boy Elijah had once sheltered." The steel box chain isn't visible.
- **Blocking lock:** Marcus **stays standing** at the porch rail, with his back to the street. He never sits down, and that physical distance is the scene.
- **Expressions:** reassuring professional confidence on his line, then no answer to Elijah. His jaw sets, and he looks away toward the street.

### FL-MR32: Marcus Reynolds, 32 (condo kitchen, January 2023)
- **Wardrobe (continuity from PART 4):** suit pants and a white dress shirt, sleeves loosened. The tie and jacket were "discarded hours ago." The collar is open and his watch is still on.
- **State:** exhausted. Faint smoke haze from the burned eggs still hangs in the pendant light.

## 4. Light locks

### LK-2022-AM: the porch, late March 2022, 9:30–10:00 AM (LOCKED)
- **Sun:** east-southeast (about 101°–106°), 27°–33° high. That's low enough to reach under the porch roof and across the floorboards to Elijah's face in his chair. By 11:00 AM (46° high) his face would be in the roof's shadow.
- **Key light:** direct morning sun, about 5000K. It's soft because the Tampa humidity haze diffuses it: bright, but not gold like 2006.
- **Direction on faces:** Elijah faces the street, so he's lit **from the front**. Marcus stands with his back to the street, so he's **backlit**: a bright rim and his face in soft bounce light. He has literally turned his back on the light.
- **Fill:** bounce off the cream clapboard wall and the painted porch ceiling.
- **Grade:** a memory look, but **less warm than 2006**. Gentle highlight bloom, light humid haze, and fine grain at half the strength of the 2006 grain. It's a recent memory, not a childhood one.

### LK-ATL-NIGHT: the condo kitchen, January 2023, about 10:30 PM (NEW)
- **Practicals:** the single pendant over the island (2700K, warm pool), under-cabinet LED strips (3000K, low), and the range hood light off.
- **Window:** the Atlanta skyline at night gives a cool blue ambient light with warm specks of city light. There's no dusk left in the sky.
- **Atmosphere:** the leftover smoke from the burned eggs makes a faint haze that catches the pendant light, so you can see the cone of light.
- **Grade:** low-key, neutral-cool, deep blacks, no grain. It's the loneliest light in the episode.
- **Plate:** night relight `8735e56d-c49f-47d2-b135-7c01080f3718`, made from the dusk plate `f5da915e…` so the room stays the same. Use it for PART 4's kitchen beat too.

## 6. PART 5 "The Porch": script (LOCKED)

*Episode button. Also stands alone as a short-form clip. Estimated runtime 2:15–2:45. Dialogue is verbatim from the locked chapter, including Elijah's last two lines (Decision 2).*

> **FLASHBACK — EXT. YBOR CITY SAFE HOUSE, FRONT PORCH — MORNING — LATE MARCH 2022**
>
> `[S01]` THE PORCH from the front walk. Morning sun lies low across the floorboards. ELIJAH JOHNSON (77) sits in his rocking chair facing the street, the way it always faces the street, a newspaper in his lap. He looks older than we've ever seen him.
>
> `[S02]` INSERT: the newspaper, gone grey and soft in the humidity. Headline: **"FLORIDA RETURNS TO DARK AGES WITH ANTI-LGBTQ LEGISLATION."**
>
> `[S03]` From behind Elijah's chair, looking out at the bright street: MARCUS (31), navy suit, no tie, sunglasses in hand, comes up the porch steps out of the sun. A board **CREAKS** under him (the third of the house's four).
>
> `[S04]` Elijah doesn't look up yet.
>
> ELIJAH
> Feels like 2005 all over again. Except I had more fight in me back then.
>
> `[S05]` Marcus at the rail, his back to the street, the sun behind him. Composed and certain.
>
> MARCUS
> This will be challenged in court. There are solid constitutional arguments against it.
>
> `[S06]` Elijah finally looks up and gives him **a long look.** We hold on it.
>
> `[S07]` The camera slowly pushes in on Elijah.
>
> ELIJAH
> Laws matter, Marcus. But so do hearts. While you're fighting in courtrooms, who's fighting in living rooms? In classrooms? Who's telling these kids they deserve to exist when their own state is saying they don't?
> This house has been answering that question a lot longer than either of us has been alive to ask it.
> *(beat)*
> Somebody's got to keep the porch light on.
>
> `[S08]` Marcus doesn't answer. His jaw sets. He looks away, out toward the street and the light.
>
> `[S09]` The two of them from the end of the porch: Elijah returns to his paper, and Marcus stays standing. The space between them holds.
>
> `[S10]` INSERT: Marcus's hand tightens on the porch rail.

**MATCH CUT TO:**

**INT. MARCUS'S CONDO — KITCHEN — ATLANTA — NIGHT — CONTINUOUS (PRESENT)**

`[S11]` INSERT: the same hand, in the same spot in the frame, now gripping the cold quartzite counter edge. The warm memory grade cuts hard to cool.

`[S12]` WIDE: Marcus alone, leaning back against the counter. A thin haze of smoke from the burned eggs drifts through the cone of the pendant light. Beyond the window, the city is dark and glittering.

MARCUS (V.O.)
Marcus Reynolds, Esquire. On the verge of partnership. Respected by colleagues and clients alike. About to come face to face with the past he'd spent seventeen years trying to outrun.

`[S13]` CLOSE on Marcus, with the city lights behind him in the black window glass. Beat.

`[S14]` The camera slowly pulls away, back through the kitchen, until Marcus is small in the frame and the pendant light is the only warm thing left.

MARCUS (V.O.) (CONT'D)
And Elijah — dear, wise, unflinching Elijah — wouldn't be there to help him through it this time.

**FADE OUT. END OF EPISODE 1.**

## 9. Decisions (ALL APPROVED by Sire, 2026-09-24)

1. **Porch time: 9:30–10:00 AM, late March 2022.** That's the only morning window when the sun is low enough to reach Elijah's face under the porch roof. One word changed in the expanded chapter (a line I wrote): **"Late morning, still soft"** became **"Mid-morning, still soft."** **Approved.**
2. **Add Elijah's two extra lines to PART 5.** They were already in the locked expanded chapter but missing from the script. "Somebody's got to keep the porch light on" echoes PART 3's opening ("as if the porch light had already known his name"), and it's a strong last line for Elijah in the episode. **Approved.**
3. **History fix in the chapter's narration (Sire's original text).** The chapter says Elijah was "referring to the year Tampa Pride was banned." In reality, the 2005 ban stopped Hillsborough County's government from recognizing or promoting Pride; the event itself wasn't banned. The fix, now in chapter v3: *"referring to the year the county commission banned its own government from so much as acknowledging Pride."* Elijah's dialogue doesn't change either way. **Approved: fixed in chapter v3.**
4. **Porch creak map (series continuity).** The chapter says the porch "creaked in exactly four places." Lock: creak 1 = the bottom step and creak 2 = the top step (both heard in PART 3), creak 3 = the board in front of the door (PART 5), and **creak 4 = saved for a later episode**. **Approved.**
5. **Marcus at 31 wardrobe:** navy suit, open collar, no tie, sunglasses in hand. **Approved.**
6. **Run the two builds in Section 8.** **Approved and built.**
