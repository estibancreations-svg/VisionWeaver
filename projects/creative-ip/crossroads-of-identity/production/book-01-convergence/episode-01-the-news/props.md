# Props — Episode 1 "The News"

Production record for Episode 1's hero props. They were first used in PART 4 ("The Box"). Shot rules are in [`part-04/lock-sheet.md`](part-04/lock-sheet.md). Every prop is **original art and design**. The magazine is our own invented title, and no real publication, brand or logo is copied.

## Hero props (these must stay identical across the series)

- **The carved figure:** a small hand-carved wooden figure, about 5 in tall, of a seated man reading an open book. It's dark wood, worn smooth and glossy where a thumb rests on the book's spine. **A series hero prop.** Board `6345f830-e173-4501-aa1f-b86879c44147`.
- **The photograph:** a 4×6 print from summer 2001, slightly faded and warm-toned, with soft corners. Prop `10523301-c6cb-457b-81ee-33fef5eaee58`.
- **Grandmother's Bible:** worn black leather with gilt page edges. The inside cover has handwritten, faded ink: *Marcus Reynolds · Born January 9, 1991*.
- **The debate medal:** a bronze medal on a faded blue ribbon. The engraving is too small to read.
- **The shoebox:** a plain white/grey cardboard shoebox with no brand, soft at the corners.
- **The magazine: *CITYGRAIN*** ("Atlanta · Homes · Design · Culture"), **our own invented title and design**. A search found no existing magazine with that name. It replaces "Atlanta Modern Living" (script) and "Tampa Bay Modern Living" (chapter), which sat too close to real titles like *Tampa Bay Living* and *Modern Luxury*. The cover is his own living room, headed "Rising Legal Star's Urban Oasis," Spring 2022. Prop `47ccde9b-88b0-4916-9e7a-f482bd12ade3`.
- **Phone:** his modern smartphone. His mother's contact label is **"Mother"**. It's formal and distant, which says a lot about them.
