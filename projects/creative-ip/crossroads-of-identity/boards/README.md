## Desiree Washington (FL-DW): how the board was made

- **Source:** Sire's original portrait (2026-09-26): copper-auburn corkscrew curls with a center part, warm golden-brown skin, deep-brown eyes, calm serious expression, grey linen wrap robe. It replaces the earlier Desiree looks (the "Sarah Miller" source board and the "365-degree blueprint"), per Sire: *"Here is her original image. Create her board and do the 360 version."*
- **v1 (40 credits):** written description only, because the portrait couldn't be sent from this sandbox. **v2 (40 credits):** after Sire uploaded the portrait to Runway, rebuilt with the portrait itself as the reference, so the face should now match.
- **Filled in (not in the portrait, which is chest-up):** wide-leg charcoal linen trousers and flat tan leather sandals to complete the head-to-toe views; height 5'7" (170 cm) from her earlier board. **No jewelry**, matching the portrait.
- **Age:** canon has her born in 1988, so she's about 35 in Episode 1's January 2023.
- **Layout:** the 360 View Storyboard standard: 8 head-to-toe views (front, front-right, right, back-right, back, back-left, left, front-left) in two rows of four, same light and scale on all 8, plus face, hands and fabric close-ups.
