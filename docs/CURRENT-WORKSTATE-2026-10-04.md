## World State / Scene Dissection
The existing Boy and Red Balloon material remains the test bed. Preserve all accepted footage and references.

The world model must account for:
- geography and persistent zones;
- visible and off-screen entities;
- acoustic sources (including unresolved off-screen sources);
- traffic, phones, babies, water, garbage trucks and other ambient activity;
- location-appropriate birds/insects/animals;
- weather, wind, rain, light and reflections;
- camera position, movement and motivated reframing;
- causality, object movement, reactions and aftermath;
- uncertainty rather than inventing facts not established by the source clip.

The goal is a persistent 360-degree world, not a flat frame.
