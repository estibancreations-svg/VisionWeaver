# Commercial Creativity Station — First Production Test
**Date:** 2026-10-06
**Brands:** Pepsi Zero Sugar / Golden Corral
**Purpose:** first authored test run after Commercial Creativity Station baseline.

## A. Pepsi Zero Sugar — "Kick the Crave" v2
**Template:** Beverage × Craving → Product × Cinematic
**Master:** 30 seconds
**Core claim boundary:** zero sugar / zero calories / bold Pepsi taste. Do not invent superiority, pricing, availability or campaign affiliation.

### Board
1. 0:00–0:04 — CRAVE
   Extreme close food details: wings glaze, burger sear, fries dropping, game-night table.
   Sound before music: sizzle, crunch, room energy.
2. 0:04–0:08 — INTERRUPTION
   Exact Pepsi Zero Sugar can lands cold in frame.
   Condensation and label are readable; package geometry locked.
3. 0:08–0:13 — REACH
   Hand reaches. Macro glide across exact supplied label.
   Friends stay soft in background; can remains hero.
4. 0:13–0:18 — RELEASE
   Pull-tab macro. Crisp carbonation release becomes the edit transition.
   No impossible splash behavior.
5. 0:18–0:24 — EXPERIENCE
   First drink. Food + cola rhythm. Natural reaction; no exaggerated AI smiles.
6. 0:24–0:30 — MEMORY
   Exact hero packshot with condensation and controlled red/blue edge light.
   Super: KICK THE CRAVE
   Secondary: PEPSI ZERO SUGAR

### Upgrade over prior board
- craving starts before packshot
- product arrives as the story turn
- stronger sound design
- exact object remains visible through the main progression
- human reaction is secondary to sensory product proof
- final frame matches the master can reference

### Hard QC
Reject label warping, wrong can proportions, inconsistent condensation, impossible hand contact, logo deformation, duplicated faces, floating food, or continuity breaks.

## B. Golden Corral — "Everybody Gets Their Favorite"
**Template:** Family Dining × Choose Your Favorite × Cinematic
**Master:** 30 seconds
**Commercial thesis:** the restaurant becomes the place where one family can want completely different things and still share one dinner.

### Board
1. 0:00–0:04 — THE QUESTION
   Family of five arrives at a warm dining room.
   Daughter: "What are you getting?"
   Fast looks around the table.
2. 0:04–0:09 — THE CHOICE
   Move into buffet line.
   Steak grill, fried chicken, pot roast, vegetables, salad, fresh rolls.
   Camera treats each food station as a hero, not background.
3. 0:09–0:15 — THE BUILD
   Plates become different personalities:
   - grandfather: sirloin + vegetables
   - mother: pot roast + mashed potatoes
   - father: fried chicken + sides
   - teenager: mixed plate
   - child: mac & cheese + roll
   No plate magically changes between cuts.
4. 0:15–0:20 — THE TABLE
   Server places drinks.
   Family sits down together.
   Steam, bread tear, knife through steak, gravy movement.
5. 0:20–0:25 — THE PAYOFF
   Conversation stops for a beat as everyone starts eating.
   Child looks around: "Everybody got what they wanted."
   Small family laugh.
6. 0:25–0:30 — THE MEMORY FRAME
   Wide table hero shot with the restaurant environment still visible.
   VO: "One table. A lot of favorites."
   End super: GOLDEN CORRAL
   Brand line: THE ONLY ONE FOR EVERYONE.

### Why this works
- uses Golden Corral's real variety as the plot
- sells family agreement instead of "cheap buffet"
- makes the dining room part of perceived quality
- puts food texture ahead of generic smiling-family footage
- gives every family member a reason to be there
- supports 30s, 15s, 9:16, 1:1, still and carousel derivatives

### Hard QC
Lock plate contents, food doneness, buffet station layout, cast wardrobe, table position, restaurant lighting, utensils, drink levels, and food states.
Reject cloned diners, over-glossed food, impossible steam, morphing plates, repeated hands, floating utensils, or generic stock-looking smiles.
